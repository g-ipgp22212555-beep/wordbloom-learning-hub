Pupil user guide

🌼 How to use WordBloom

1. Choose your year

  1. Open WordBloom using the link given by your teacher.
  2. Select Year 4 or Year 5.
  3. Choose a learning garden from the navigation menu.

2. Phonics Garden

  1. Choose your year level.
  2. Press the speaker button to hear a word.
  3. Repeat the word aloud and notice its sounds.

3. Vocabulary Garden

  1. Choose your year, theme and word category.
  2. Read each word and its example sentence.
  3. Choose “I know it” or “Practise again” to record your practice.
  4. Answer the quick word check and read the feedback.

4. Word Wall

  1. Explore the three curriculum themes.
  2. Read the words in each collection.
  3. Use the speaking challenge and sentence frame to describe something.

5. StoryBloom

  1. Choose your year level and picture idea.
  2. Complete the beginning, middle and ending boxes.
  3. Select “Build my story” and read your story aloud.
  4. Print your learning record if your teacher asks you to.
Need help? Ask your teacher or a trusted adult if a button, sound or page does not work on your device.
Curriculum connection

📘 WordBloom and the DSKP

WordBloom uses the KSSR (Semakan 2017) English DSKP for Year 4 and Year 5 as its curriculum reference. Activities are designed for practice and can provide learning evidence for teacher review; they do not automatically award official Performance Levels.

Curriculum levels and themes

Year 4: CEFR A1 High. Year 5: CEFR A2 Low.

Both syllabuses use three broad themes: World of Self, Family and Friends; World of Stories; and World of Knowledge.

Additional speaking standards supported

Year 4: 2.1.1 give reasons for basic opinions; 2.1.2 describe past experiences; 2.1.3 give instructions/directions; 2.1.4 give reasons for simple predictions; 2.2.2 check steps for classroom tasks.

Year 5: 2.1.1 give detailed personal information; 2.1.2 describe experiences up to now; 2.1.3 ask for/give/respond to advice; 2.1.4 describe future plans; 2.2.2 agree on steps for classroom tasks.

Vocabulary examples are selected from the official DSKP core vocabulary wordlists. Words are presented in meaningful contexts rather than as memorisation-only lists. The DSKP wordlists are cumulative and may be adapted to local classroom context.
Our learning project

🌷 About WordBloom

Our purpose

WordBloom is a student-developed digital English learning prototype designed to provide Year 4 and Year 5 pupils with engaging opportunities to practise vocabulary, pronunciation, speaking and storytelling.

Learning journey

Recognise sounds → Explore vocabulary → Describe and interact → Create and share stories.

Curriculum references

Digital tools and attribution

This prototype was developed as a standalone web application. It uses browser speech synthesis where available, standard web technologies and original interface styling. The official DSKP documents are acknowledged as curriculum sources. Any additional images or media added by the group should be credited individually with creator/source and licence details.

WordBloom is a student-developed educational prototype. It is not an official Ministry of Education Malaysia product. It does not collect pupil accounts or send personal information to a server; practice progress and story drafts are stored locally in the browser.
